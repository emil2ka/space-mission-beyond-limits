# 0012 Asteroid and economy in M3: where the engine departed from the design — accepted (2026-11-14)

**Context:** the contract [M3 Asteroid Contract](../04%20Tech/M3%20Asteroid%20Contract.md) and the design ([Asteroid Sample Return](../03%20Design/Missions/Asteroid%20Sample%20Return.md), [Economy](../03%20Design/Economy.md)) left the flight and preparation rules open. Part A (engine) decisions:

1. **The Asteroid has 4 science slots** — the arm, the container, the sealer and the spectrometer already fill them. So the sampling site is also imaged by the **Navigation Camera** (navigation department, quality 0.6), and there are two science goals: "Asteroid composition" (needed for Perfect) and "Sampling site imaging". There is no separate goal for the Radiation Sensor. An extra science slot is a noticeable HQ upgrade.
2. **Phase length is flexible:** the cruise and the approach end on arrival (ion 140 / chemical 100 ticks; approach 80 with the navigation camera or radar, 110 without), and sampling ends earlier via the rule "Sample Secured → Start preparing the return". `missions.ts` stores the maximums. The flight is 541–590 ticks ≈ 9–10 min.
3. **Debris Alert and Arm Jam always get their turn** (they wait for another crisis to end; the cooldown does not apply to them); there are ≤ 2 general crises on the Asteroid. Otherwise a Power Drop could "eat" the slot of the mission's main crisis, and the report could not honestly talk about the shield or the dampers.
4. **Capsule entry does not cut the craft's link** — on the Asteroid, a blackout happens after dodging debris without a Star Tracker / Reaction Wheels (the antenna loses Earth for 8 ticks). The plasma affects the capsule, not the craft near the asteroid.
5. **Perfect = a sealed sample + the capsule found + composition data delivered** (and the sample not overheated). Scoring ≥ 90 without these gives Success. No sample on Earth — Partial at most ("data delivered, sample lost").
6. **Preparation (prep):** after the flight, `agency.pendingPrep = true`. There are two ways to leave the report: `confirmPrep {insurance, next}` (the full path, with insurance) or the old `goStage` ("Again" / "To HQ" on the M1 screens) — it confirms preparation **without insurance** at the price the report has already shown (`mc.upkeep`). This keeps the M1 "Again" test unchanged, and there is never a next launch without preparation.
7. **Free basic repair** = the balance is not touched, damage is cleared, and the craft is rebuilt as the mission's `STARTER_BUILD` (it passes the readiness check). Spending MC on unlocks and upgrades before preparation is allowed: if nothing is left for preparation, it is the same free repair. There is no negative balance and no soft-lock in any sequence (property test).
8. **Insurance for 15 MC** covers repairs after the **next** flight (it is bought during preparation, before the flight). Repairing a module costs 20 % of its price, at least 5 MC; the hull costs 2 MC per 10 %.
9. **`room.agency` is the source of truth**; `room.credits` / `room.unlocked` are kept as mirrors for the M1/M2 screens. `computeStats(build, mission, mods?)` got a third parameter (upgrades); the flight stores them in `flight.mods`.
10. **Upgrade trade-offs** (spec: "each one is a clear trade-off"): light alloys — debris damage ×1.25; antennas — +20 % power; batteries — +3 kg each; PDU v2 — 8 W instead of 5; extra slot — +5 kg to the hull, the department is chosen at purchase (Science by default); Flight Computer v2 — +5 W and +3 heat.

**Why:** causality from the build (0006), honest numbers in the report, no unexpected charges, no randomness except the seed.
**Alternatives:** a separate `prep` stage in the room (breaks the M1/M2 transitions and their tests); charging for preparation right after the flight (an unexpected charge — forbidden by the design); returning to an "empty" build on free repair (the team might fail to build a craft — the same soft-lock).
